Load Balancing Lab 09

The same simulation ran 2.83× faster

100,000 requests · 100 servers
Before · list1.030 s
After · deque0.364 s

Profiling led us to arrivals being removed from the front of a list. Switching to deque.popleft preserved every result. At 10,000 requests, the same change took 38.9 ms down to 34.3 ms.
